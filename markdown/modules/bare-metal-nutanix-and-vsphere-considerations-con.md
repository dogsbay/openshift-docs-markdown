# Registry storage considerations for bare metal, Nutanix, and vSphere {id="bare-metal-nutanix-and-vsphere-considerations-con_{{ context }}"}

Bare-metal, vSphere, and Nutanix installations do not provision registry storage automatically. Review the per-platform storage options, and the `Recreate` rollout strategy that block storage requires.
