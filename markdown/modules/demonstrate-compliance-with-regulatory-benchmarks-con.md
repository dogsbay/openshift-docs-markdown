# Demonstrate compliance with regulatory benchmarks {id="demonstrate-compliance-with-regulatory-benchmarks-con_{{ context }}"}

The Compliance Operator automates the inspection of numerous technical implementations and compares them against industry standards, benchmarks, and baselines. Use it to detect compliance gaps in a running cluster and to produce verifiable results for internal and external auditors.
