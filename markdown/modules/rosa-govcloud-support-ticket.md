# Opening a support ticket {id="rosa-govcloud-support-ticket_{{ context }}"}

To get access to open a support ticket, complete the following steps. {._abstract}

**Procedure**

1.  If you need to create an account, contact fedramp-css@openshiftusgov.com.
1.  After you receive access, navigate to the [Red Hat GovCloud support portal](https://redhatgov.servicenowservices.com/css).
1.  Click **Create Case** and complete the required information.
1.  Click **Submit**.
