# Adding support contacts {id="learning-getting-started-support-contacts_{{ context }}"}

You can add additional email addresses for communications about your cluster.  {._abstract}

**Procedure**

1.  On the {{ cluster_manager_first }} user interface (UI), click **Cluster List** in the side navigation tabs.
1.  Select the cluster that needs support.
1.  Click the **Support** tab.
1.  Click **Add notification contact**, and enter the additional email addresses.
