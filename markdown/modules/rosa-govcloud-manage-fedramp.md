# Changing your Red Hat FedRAMP account password {id="rosa-govcloud-manage_{{ context }}"}

To change your FedRAMP account password, you must have access to your Red&#160;Hat FedRAMP account. {._abstract}

**Procedure**

1.  Navigate to [the Red Hat FedRAMP account management page](https://sso.openshiftusgov.com/realms/redhat-external/account).
1.  Sign in with your current username and password.
1.  Under the middle box called _Account Security_, click **Signing In**.
1.  Under _Basic authentication_, select **Password**.
1.  Click **Update** and choose a password that meets the following requirements:
    *   Minimum of fifteen (15) characters
    *   At least one (1) upper-case letter
    *   At least one (1) lower-case letter
    *   At least one (1) number
    *   At least one (1) special character (e.g. ~ ! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) _ + = - ' [ ] / ? > &lt;)
1.  Confirm your password.
1.  Click **Submit**.
