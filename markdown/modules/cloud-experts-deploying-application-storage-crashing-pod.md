# Crashing the pod {id="cloud-experts-deploying-application-storage-crashing-pod_{{ context }}"}

Crash the pod to test that persistent storage persists across pod restarts. {._abstract}

**Procedure**

1.  On the OSToy app console, click **Home** in the left menu.
1.  Click **Crash pod**.
