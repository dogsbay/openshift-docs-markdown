# Configure the integration settings in 3scale {id="ossm-threescale-integration-settings_{{ context }}"}

Follow this procedure to configure the 3scale integration settings.


:::note

For 3scale SaaS customers, {{ SMProductName }} is enabled as part of the Early Access program.

:::


**Procedure**

1.  Navigate to **[your_API_name]** → **Integration**
1.  Click **Settings**.
1.  Select the **Istio** option under _Deployment_.
    *   The **API Key (user_key)** option under _Authentication_ is selected by default.
1.  Click **Update Product** to save your selection.
1.  Click **Configuration**.
1.  Click **Update Configuration**.
