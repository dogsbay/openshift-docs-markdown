# Billing and pricing {id="rosa-sdpolicy-billing_{{ context }}"}

{{ product_title }} is billed directly to your {{ AWS }} account. ROSA pricing is consumption based, with annual commitments or three-year commitments for greater discounting. The total cost of ROSA consists of two components:

*   ROSA service fees
*   AWS infrastructure fees

Visit the [{{ product_title }} Pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/rosa/pricing/) page on the AWS website for more details.
