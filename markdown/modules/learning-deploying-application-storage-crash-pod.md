# Crashing the pod {id="learning-deploying-application-storage-crash-pod_{{ context }}"}

To demonstrate the persistent storage of your cluster, cause the pod of your application to crash from the OSToy application console. {._abstract}

**Procedure**

1.  On the OSToy application console, click **Home** in the left menu.
1.  Click **Crash pod**.
