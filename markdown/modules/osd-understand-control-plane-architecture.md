# Understand control plane architecture and core services {id="osd-understand-control-plane-architecture_{{ context }}"}

The control plane is composed of control plane machines that manage your {{ product_title }} cluster and the workloads that run on its compute machines.
Understanding how machine roles, Operators, and the etcd key-value store fit together helps you evaluate what Red&#160;Hat manages on your behalf and what remains under your control.
