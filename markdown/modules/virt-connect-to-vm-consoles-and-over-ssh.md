# Connect to VM consoles and over SSH {id="virt-connect-to-vm-consoles-and-over-ssh_{{ context }}"}

Connect to a running virtual machine for troubleshooting or maintenance. You can open the VNC console, the serial console, or the desktop viewer from the web console, or connect over SSH, depending on your access needs and network environment. {._abstract}
