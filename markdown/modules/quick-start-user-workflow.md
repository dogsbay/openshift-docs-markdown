# Quick start user workflow {id="quick-start-user-workflow_{{ context }}"}

When you interact with an existing quick start tutorial, this is the expected workflow experience: {._abstract}

1.  In the **Administrator** or **Developer** perspective, click the **Help icon** and select **Quick Starts**.
1.  Click a quick start card.
1.  In the panel that is displayed, click **Start**.
1.  Complete the on-screen instructions, then click **Next**.
1.  In the **Check your work** module that is displayed, answer the question to confirm that you successfully completed the task.
    1.  If you select **Yes**, click **Next** to continue to the next task.
    1.  If you select **No**, repeat the task instructions and check your work again.
1.  Repeat steps 1 through 6 above to complete the remaining tasks in the quick start.
1.  After completing the final task, click **Close** to close the quick start.
