# Search the Red&#160;Hat Knowledgebase {id="support-knowledgebase-search_{{ context }}"}

Search the Red&#160;Hat Knowledgebase to find solutions to known issues and resolve problems quickly without opening a support case. {._abstract}

**Prerequisites**

*   You have a Red&#160;Hat Customer Portal account.

**Procedure**

1.  Log in to the [Red&#160;Hat Customer Portal](http://access.redhat.com).
1.  Click **Search**.
1.  In the search field, input keywords and strings relating to the problem, including:
    *   {{ product_title }} components (such as **etcd**)
    *   Related procedure (such as **installation**)
    *   Warnings, error messages, and other outputs related to explicit failures
1.  Click the **Enter** key.
1.  Optional: Select the **{{ product_title }}** product filter.
1.  Optional: Select the **Documentation** content type filter.
