# Adding support contacts {id="cloud-experts-getting-started-support-add-contact_{{ context }}"}

You can add additional email addresses for communications about your cluster.  {._abstract}

**Procedure**

1.  On the {{ cluster_manager_first }} user interface (UI), click **select cluster**.
1.  Click the **Support** tab.
1.  Click **Add notification contact**, and enter the additional email addresses.
